About Us

Protea Care Medical (Pty) Ltd

Protea Care Medical (Pty) Ltd is a healthcare practice dedicated to providing professional, compassionate and comprehensive medical care to individuals, women and families.

Our Mission

To provide professional, compassionate and comprehensive healthcare to individuals, women and families through patient-focused medical services, health education, preventive care and ongoing medical support.

Our Vision

To be a trusted healthcare practice recognised for delivering quality medical care, supporting healthier families and contributing positively to the wellbeing of the communities we serve.

Company Overview

The practice brings together a broad range of general medical and women's healthcare services under one business, with an emphasis on professional service, compassion and comprehensive care.

Led by medical practitioner Dr M Kazadi, Protea Care Medical serves a healthcare purpose centred on individuals, women and families.

Our Core Values

Professionalism

We value professional conduct, responsible medical practice and a commitment to quality healthcare.

Compassion

We recognise the importance of treating every patient with dignity, respect and understanding.

Comprehensive Care

We aim to address a broad range of healthcare needs through consultations, preventive services, and patient support.

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

Protea Care Medical (Pty) Ltd offers a full range of professional medical services as listed below.

Family Planning

Providing patients with access to professional medical assessment and advice regarding general health concerns.

Preventive Healthcare

Focusing on maintaining health, identifying potential medical concerns, and encouraging early intervention.

Minor Procedures

Ultrasound & Medical Tests

Diagnostic ultrasound scans and necessary medical tests included in the practice's service offering.
