Our Medical Offerings

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

Professional medical care tailored to support individuals and families across all stages of life.

Antenatal Care — BANC Plus

Supporting the health and wellbeing of pregnant women throughout pregnancy with structured maternal healthcare.

Women's Health

Addressing healthcare needs specific to women, supporting medical assessment, health maintenance, and appropriate care.

Family Planning

Supporting patients in making informed decisions about reproductive health and pregnancy planning.

HIV Testing and Treatment

Supporting HIV screening, appropriate clinical care, and ongoing treatment management with professional confidentiality.

Chronic Disease Management

Supporting patients living with long-term medical conditions through assessment, monitoring, and ongoing medical care.

General Medical Consultations

Providing patients with access to professional medical assessment and advice regarding general health concerns.

Preventive Healthcare

Focusing on maintaining health, identifying potential medical concerns, and encouraging early intervention.

Minor Procedures

Safe clinical minor procedures performed on-site. Specific procedures can be confirmed directly with the practice.

Ultrasound & Medical Tests

Diagnostic ultrasound scans and necessary medical tests to support accurate clinical evaluation and treatment plans.
